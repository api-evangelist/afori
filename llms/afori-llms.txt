# Afori
> **Afori is an AI software platform built specifically for insurance brokers in Germany.**  
> It provides specialized AI assistants (KI-Helfer) that automate insurance back-office processes, structure incoming communication, and convert unstructured emails and documents into actionable, traceable cases.  
>  
> Rather than generating isolated text responses, Afori produces review-ready work results — structured cases with tasks and reminders, context-aware email drafts, and prefilled claim notifications.  
>  
> The platform integrates seamlessly into Microsoft Outlook and broker management systems (MVP), understands insurance-specific processes and terminology, and operates within the European regulatory framework (DSGVO, IDD, EU AI Act).  
>  
> **DSGVO-compliant. EU-hosted. ISO 27001:2022 certified.**

- [Platform Overview](https://www.afori.com/en/plattform/ueberblick)  
  The intelligent AI platform for brokers. Revolutionize brokerage processes with automation — from email classification to claims handling, seamlessly integrated.
 
- [Task Management](https://www.afori.com/en/plattform/aufgabenmanagement)  
  Manage your tasks smarter and faster. Incoming data such as emails, notes, and documents are automatically linked to active cases and converted into actionable tasks.
 
- [AI Agents](https://www.afori.com/en/plattform/ki-helfer)  
  Specialized AI agents handle back-office work — from personalized email drafts and client inquiries to insurer document checks — for more time with clients.
 
- [AI Assistant](https://www.afori.com/en/plattform/ki-assistent)  
  Your personal insurance assistant: GDPR-compliant, equipped with deep insurance expertise, and always aware of all ongoing cases.
 
- [Integrations](https://www.afori.com/en/plattform/integrationen)  
  Afori runs inside the tools brokers already use, with no system migration required. It connects directly to the leading Maklerverwaltungsprogramme (MVP, broker management systems) on the German market: AMS, VEMA, FiRST CRM, ProClient, Professional Works, CORPIT, and VIAS. It also integrates with the everyday tools in a broker's workflow, including the comparison platforms NAFI and Comparit. This breadth across the existing broker software stack means low switching friction and fast time to value.
 
- [Security](https://www.afori.com/en/plattform/sicherheit)  
  Maximum security for your data. Hosted in Europe, GDPR compliant, ISO 27001:2022 certified, end-to-end encrypted.
